# Customer-Profitability
Customer Behavior and Sales Analysis (businesses understand behavior, optimize pricing, and improve marketing strategies).

# Project Overview
Businesses collect large amounts of customer and sales data, but many still treat all customers as equally valuable. Some customers bring in most of the profit, while others cost the business money. Our goal here is to identify and understand the factors that make some customers generate more profit than others.​

# Objectives
Analyze Customer characteristics, purchase behavior, product preference and profitability.​

# Audience 
Management and business owners, Marketing team, Sales, Customer teams​

# Scope
Analysis of customer and sales data, Interactive Dashboard and Reporting
